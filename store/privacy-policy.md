# TradeHours Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

TradeHours ("the app") is a personal hours-tracking app.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

**Where your data lives:** the hours, jobsite names, and travel/parking notes you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your phone unless you choose to copy them and paste them somewhere yourself.

**Permissions:** the app requests no permissions. It has no internet access.

**Ads, analytics, and tracking:** none. There are no advertising or analytics libraries in the app.

**Backups:** if you have Android's device backup enabled, your phone's operating system may back up the app's data to your own Google account, under your Google account's privacy settings. We do not have access to it.

**Children:** the app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

**Deleting your data:** uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

**Changes:** if this policy changes it will be updated on this page.
